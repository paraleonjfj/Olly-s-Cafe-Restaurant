OUR STORY

A PLACE TO ENJOY

Welcome to Olly's Lucena City Branch! This page is dedicated to introducing our store and what visitors can discover when they come by.

Olly's Lucena City Branch brings a cozy, welcoming dining experience to the heart of Lucena, Quezon.

From hearty Filipino breakfast plates and comfort food classics to handmade pasta, pizza, and freshly baked cakes, our menu is made to please every craving, whether you're here for a quick bite, a family meal, or a celebration.

Start your morning with our silog-style plates and toasts, share crispy wings and starters with friends, enjoy a steak or rice bowl for lunch, and finish with a slice of cheesecake and a cold drink.

OUR GOAL

GOOD FOOD THAT FEELS LIKE HOME

We aim to serve delicious, high-quality meals at fair prices, in a comfortable space where every guest feels welcome.
